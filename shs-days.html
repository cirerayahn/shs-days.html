Senior High School Experiences

Holy Cross of Carigara

My alma mater is Holy Cross of Carigara. My Senior High School days were not always the happiest times in my life. There were challenges and moments that felt depressing.

Events and Group Activities

Even though it was a tough period, I still took part in many events and group activities. These moments helped me grow and stay connected with my classmates.

What I Participated In

  • Dance performances
  • Singing activities
  • Various school events
  • Group projects and presentations
Work Immersion

One of the most memorable parts of my SHS life was my Work Immersion. I was assigned to a police station. I really enjoyed the experience and learned a lot from it.

Reflection

Looking back, SHS had its ups and downs. The group activities and the work immersion at the police station were the parts I appreciated the most.

  1. Joined dance and singing activities
  2. Participated in many group events
  3. Completed work immersion at a police station
  4. Learned the value of teamwork and responsibility
